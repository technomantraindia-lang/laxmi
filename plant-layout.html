PLANT AT A GLANCE · 8-STAGE PRODUCTION SEQUENCE

One plant.
One connected production flow.

An AAC plant works as a connected sequence. Each production stage has a defined role, position and relationship with the stages around it.

STAGE 01

Raw Material

Fly ash, cement, lime & gypsum storage, conveying and weighing

STAGE 02

Slurry Prep

Wet grinding of fly ash into fine slurry in ball mill

STAGE 03

Batching

PLC-controlled dosing, mixing & aluminium addition

STAGE 04

Pouring

Slurry pouring into oiled precision steel moulds

STAGE 05

Precuring

Temperature-controlled rising tunnel at 40–50°C

STAGE 06

Cutting

3-stage oscillating wire cutting with ±1mm accuracy

STAGE 07

Autoclave

12-bar saturated steam curing at 190°C for 10–12 hrs

STAGE 08

Packing

Auto separation, stacking & shrink-wrap dispatch

STAGE 01

RAW MATERIAL PREPARATION

WHAT HAPPENS

Incoming fly ash or silica sand is milled with water in heavy ball mills to produce a fine, homogeneous slurry of uniform density.

RELATED EQUIPMENT

Wet Ball Mill & Slurry Storage Tanks

PLANT POSITION

Front-end material yard adjacent to raw material hoppers and slurry storage tanks.
