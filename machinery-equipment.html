01 / STAGE

Material preparation

Start with the front end of the line. High-efficiency wet grinding mills and automated slurry preparation systems ensuring process continuity.

01 · MILLING

BALL MILL

Heavy-duty wet grinding ball mill designed for precise particle size reduction of siliceous material (fly ash or sand slurry) with rubber lining for long service life.

High capacity wet grinding • Rubber lining
02 · SLURRY

SLURRY MAKING SYSTEM

High-shear slurry preparation tanks equipped with heavy agitators for thorough mixing of fly ash, sand, and lime slurry to reach optimal density.

High-shear agitator • Density control
03 · STORAGE

SLURRY STORAGE TANKS

Vertical slurry storage tanks with continuous low-speed agitation to prevent particle sedimentation and guarantee uninterrupted 24/7 slurry feed to batching.

Continuous agitation • Anti-sedimentation
02 / STAGE

Batching, moulding & precuring

Automated load-cell dosing, high-speed slurry mixing, precision mould box sealing, and temperature-controlled precuring tunnels.

01 · DOSING

AUTOMATED BATCHING UNIT

PLC-controlled weighing scales and dosing computer for precise metering of slurry, cement, lime, gypsum, and aluminum powder slurry.

PLC Load cell • ±0.5% Accuracy
02 · MOULDING

MOULD BOX & SIDE PLATES

Precision-machined structural steel mould boxes with leak-proof oil seals and detachable side plates engineered for heavy thermal cycle endurance.

Precision machined • Leak-proof seals
03 · PRECURING

PRECURING RISING TUNNELS

Insulated, heat-retaining rising chambers designed for consistent green cake fermentation and controlled strength development before cutting.

Heat-retaining • Controlled rising
03 / STAGE

High-precision cutting & handling

The core visual moment of the AAC plant: zero-waste wire cutting machines and automated heavy cake manipulation cranes.

01 · CUTTING

ADVANCED THREE-STAGE & VERTICAL CUTTER

High-speed oscillating wire cutting machine providing horizontal, vertical, and cross cuts for exact block dimensions with smooth surface finish.

Oscillating wire • ±1mm Dimension tolerance
02 · TILTING

TILTING CRANE & TABLE

90-degree hydraulic tilting table and crane system for gentle green cake demoulding, side-plate removal, and waste reduction.

90° Hydraulic tilt • Gentle demoulding
03 · GROUPING

AUTOMATIC GROUPING CRANE

Synchronized overhead cake grouping crane that organizes green cake blocks onto steam trolleys ready for autoclave curing.

Overhead sync • Autoclave bed loader
04 / STAGE

Autoclaving & auto packing line

Hydrothermal curing under high-pressure steam and fully automated block separating, palletizing, and shrink-wrapping.

01 · STEAM CURING

HEAVY INDUSTRIAL AUTOCLAVE VESSELS

ASME/IS certified high-pressure thermal autoclaves operating at 12 bar pressure and 190°C steam temperature to form tobermorite crystalline strength.

12 Bar pressure • 190°C Thermal curing
02 · PACKAGING

AUTOMATIC PACKING LINE

High-speed block separating table, automatic wooden palletizer, cube stacker, and heavy-duty plastic film shrink-wrap strapping line.

Auto separator • Palletizer & Strapping
