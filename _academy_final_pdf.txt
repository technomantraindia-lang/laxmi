04 / CONTENT ARCHITECTURE
The Academy should feel like guidance,
not a blog archive.
Keep the page focused on decisions. The homepage previews the journey; inner pages carry the depth.
What the main page shows
The investor problem in one sentence.
The five-stage decision journey.
A brief explanation of what each stage helps the
investor decide.
A visual transition into practical planning.
A trust section linking the educational path to
Laxmi's engineering responsibility.
A final CTA to continue the Academy or start a
project conversation.
What belongs on inner pages
Clear explanations and decision frameworks.
Useful visual examples, diagrams, plant layouts
and process relationships.
Tables/checklists only where they improve an
investor's decision.
Strong internal links to Solutions, Engineering
Center and Machinery.
A practical CTA at the end of every stage.
DESIGN RULE
No card wall.
No generic blog feed.
No stock-photo collage.
No forced statistics.
The visual language should match the wider Laxmi site:
premium editorial spacing, strong typography, technical
lines, industrial imagery and restrained motion.
