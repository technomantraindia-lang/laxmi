# Laxmi En-Fab — About Us Page
## Complete Multi-Section Design + Content + SEO Blueprint

**Page:** `about-us.html`  
**Company:** Laxmi En-Fab Pvt. Ltd.  
**Tagline:** Build your AAC plant with confidence.

---

## 1. Purpose

Build a premium, editorial About Us page that answers one question:

> **Who is Laxmi En-Fab, and why should a customer trust the company behind the plant and machinery?**

This is not a generic corporate profile. Avoid:
- generic mission/vision card grids
- stock office/team photography
- unsupported statistics
- long corporate-history paragraphs
- repetitive “quality / innovation / excellence” copy

Use the approved site style:
- large typography
- strong industrial photography
- generous whitespace
- asymmetric layouts
- alternating light/dark sections
- minimal cards
- thin technical lines
- subtle scroll motion
- real company/project evidence

The page should feel like a documentary about the engineering company, not a brochure pasted into HTML.
