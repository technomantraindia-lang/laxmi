01 · CORE ENGINEERING PRINCIPLE

Total system balance. Never size a plant by autoclave count alone.

A plant is only as fast as its slowest sub-system. If your slurry batching takes 7 minutes per cycle, or your pre-curing tunnel cannot release moulds every 4 minutes, adding extra autoclaves will not increase your daily block output.

PRINCIPLE 01 · PRACTICAL YIELD EQUATION

True Daily Block Yield Calculation

Daily Yield (m³) = (60 / T_cycle) × V_mould × H_eff × (1 - R_waste)

Practical output is determined by mixer cycle frequency ($T_{cycle} \approx 3.5\text{ min}$), usable mould volume ($V_{mould} = 4.0–6.0\text{ m³}$), effective operating hours ($H_{eff}$), and green cake cut scrap recovery.

PRINCIPLE 02 · 5-STAGE SYNCHRONIZATION

Synchronized Sub-System Matching

Batching (4 min) ↔ Pre-Cure (150 min) ↔ Cutting (3.5 min) ↔ Autoclave (11 hr) ↔ Boiler TPH

Every station must match the target pace. Adding autoclaves without matching steam boiler rating (TPH) causes steam starvation during concurrent vessel pressurization, degrading block strength.

03 · DUE DILIGENCE

8 verification checkpoints. Validating equipment quotes before procurement.

Critical technical questions every investor and engineering team must ask before finalizing capacity contracts and placing machinery orders.

CHECK 01 · DOSING

Usable Slurry Volume vs Gross Tank

Verify whether the machinery quote specifies net usable slurry per discharge or just total tank volume without freeboard allowance.

Metric: Net Usable m³ Freeboard Buffer
CHECK 02 · CUTTING SPEED

Cutting Machine Cycle Speed

Ensure the cutting line cycle (tilting, wire pass, vertical cross-cut, demoulding) takes less than 3.5 minutes per cake to prevent line queueing.

Target: <3.5 min/cake 2-Axis Oscillation
CHECK 03 · MOULD COUNT

Total Active Mould Quantity

Calculate moulds required: $\text{Moulds} = (\text{Pre-curing Time} / \text{Casting Cycle}) + \text{Circulation Buffer}$ to avoid pouring downtime.

Buffer: +15% Reserve Track Synchronization
CHECK 04 · STEAM BOILER

Steam Boiler Peak Load TPH

Ensure boiler rating handles peak concurrent autoclave pressurization without causing steam starvation in already soaking vessels.

Peak Demand: Concurrent Rise Steam Header Buffer
CHECK 05 · BALL MILL

Fly Ash & Sand Slurry Milling Rate

Check that the wet ball mill output (TPH) replenishes slurry storage faster than maximum peak batching consumption.

Fineness: <15% / 45µm Continuous Output
CHECK 06 · RAIL SPEED

Crane & Ferry Cart Handling Speed

Verify that transfer cars and overhead cranes can service both the pouring track and the de-panning track without traffic collisions.

Transfer: <90 sec PLC Anti-Collision
CHECK 07 · PACKAGING

Packaging & De-panning Rate

Ensure cured block cake separation and pallet strapping throughput matches continuous autoclave discharge rates (50–60 m³/hr).

Rate: 60+ m³/hr Zero Edge Chipping
CHECK 08 · SEASONAL SHIFT

Seasonal Pre-Curing Variations

Account for winter temperature drops which can extend pre-curing times from 120 mins up to 180 mins if tunnels lack heating insulation.

Winter Buffer: +45 min Heated Tunnel Option
