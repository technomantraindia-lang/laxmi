01 · Understand

A bankable AAC project is a business case.
A quotation is one input.

An AAC loan proposal must explain how the factory will be built, commissioned, operated, sold out and repaid.

Engineering assumptions and financial assumptions must describe the same project.

01

Promoter contribution

The promoter’s share of eligible project expenditure and costs not financed by the lender.

02

Term loan

Eligible fixed assets such as plant, machinery, approved civil works and utilities.

03

Working capital

The operating cycle: raw materials, production, stock and receivables, less eligible creditors.

The central principle

Banks do not finance installed capacity.
They evaluate repayment capacity.

Installed output

More machinery can create financial weight before demand exists.

Debt, depreciation, infrastructure and working capital may rise while early-stage utilisation remains low.

Saleable output

Stable production and realistic sales create the cash flow that services debt.

Capacity must reflect achievable utilisation, quality yield, product mix and regional market absorption.

“What can this plant produce, sell, deliver and collect—reliably enough to repay its obligations?”

Investor questions

Frequently asked.
Directly answered.

01 Can a new AAC plant obtain bank finance? +

Potentially, yes. Approval depends on project viability, promoter profile, contribution, security, approvals, credit history and lender policy. No machinery supplier or consultant can guarantee sanction.

02 How much promoter contribution is required? +

There is no universal percentage for every AAC project. The lender determines the margin after assessing the project, borrower and proposed security.

03 Is working capital included in the term loan? +

Working capital is normally assessed separately, even where facilities are processed together. The final structure depends on the lender.

04 Can CGTMSE make the project collateral-free? +

CGTMSE may provide guarantee cover for eligible facilities through member lenders, subject to the current scheme. It is not an automatic approval or a universal waiver of security.

05 Does a DPR guarantee loan approval? +

No. A DPR supports appraisal, but sanction and disbursement remain subject to independent credit assessment and fulfilment of lender conditions.

06 Should the plant be designed for future expansion? +

Where demand and land permit, expansion-ready planning can reduce later disruption. Phase 1 should remain financially viable without relying on unconfirmed future growth.
