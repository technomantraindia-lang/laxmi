CRITICAL PROCESS BENCHMARK · TOBERMORITE CRYSTAL SYNTHESIS

AAC gains its structural strength and low drying shrinkage exclusively from the synthesis of crystalline Tobermorite ($5CaO \cdot 6SiO_2 \cdot 5H_2O$). This hydrothermal reaction strictly requires saturated steam temperature $\ge 185^\circ\text{C}$ ($11\text{–}13\text{ bar}$) for a minimum of 6 hours. Incomplete curing leaves amorphous C-S-H gel, resulting in high moisture absorption, micro-cracking, and low compressive strength.

02 · HYDROTHERMAL CURING PROFILE

4-stage automated steam cycle. Vacuum air removal to inter-vessel steam transfer.

The autoclave curing cycle is controlled by Siemens automated PLC recipe controllers with proportional modulating steam valves that precisely track the pressure ramp-up curve.

STAGE 01

Vacuum De-aeration

0.5 hr vacuum extraction (-0.06 MPa) pulls trapped cold air out of block pore structures, allowing instant steam penetration without thermal lag.

STAGE 02

Pressure Ramp-Up

1.5–2.0 hr gradual steam injection raising vessel pressure smoothly to 12.0 bar (190°C), avoiding rapid expansion thermal shocks.

STAGE 03

Constant Soaking

6.0–8.0 hr continuous hydrothermal reaction at 12–13 bar, synthesizing the crystalline tobermorite matrix across every block layer.

STAGE 04

Steam Heat Recovery

1.0–1.5 hr blowdown transfer where 25–30% of high-pressure exhaust steam is piped into a cold autoclave to pre-heat new cakes.

03 · SAFETY & ENERGY RECOVERY

Dual fail-safe door interlocks. Statutory IBR compliance & heat exchange headers.

Autoclaves store massive potential energy under steam pressure. Laxmi vessels integrate redundant mechanical, pneumatic, and electrical interlocks to guarantee absolute operational safety.

01
Mechanical Gear-Ring Locking Teeth
Forged alloy steel gear-tooth locking ring mechanically grips the door perimeter with a safety factor of >5.0 against design pressure.
02
Pneumatic Zero-Pressure Interlock
A mechanical pilot plunger physically blocks door rotation until internal vessel pressure drops below 0.01 bar, verified by triple redundant pressure switches.
03
Inter-Autoclave Steam Cross-Header
Automated manifold connecting adjacent autoclaves enables direct steam equalisation during blowdown, shaving boiler fuel costs by up to 30%.
04
Continuous Condensate Siphon Drainage
Bottom longitudinal drainage troughs and heavy-duty thermodynamic steam traps continuously evacuate condensate, preventing bottom block soaking.
