Pneumatic / Hydraulic Safety Interlock Doors

Equipped with mechanical gear-ring locking teeth and dual pneumatic zero-pressure interlocks: door cannot physically open while residual steam pressure exceeds 0.01 bar.

Automated Steam Recovery System

During depressurization blowdown, high-pressure exhaust steam is transferred directly into an adjacent cold autoclave to pre-heat new cakes, reducing boiler fuel consumption by 25–30%.
