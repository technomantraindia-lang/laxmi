05 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Project consultation & plant setup guidance.

Clear answers to the most common questions prospective plant owners and project managers ask our engineering team.

How quickly can I get a customized CAPEX quote and preliminary plant layout? +
Once you submit your preliminary project parameters (target daily m³, plot area, and state location), our technical team delivers an initial CAPEX cost schedule and equipment list within 24 hours. If plot dimensions are shared, a customized 2D civil CAD layout is completed within 48 hours.
Do you perform raw material testing on our local fly ash and lime before machinery order? +
Yes. You can courier raw material samples (fly ash, quicklime, gypsum, aluminium powder) to our Ahmedabad lab. We perform fineness sieve tests, chemical composition checks, and autoclave sample trials to establish the optimal batching recipe and temperature curves before finalized equipment manufacturing.
Can we visit an operational Laxmi En-Fab AAC plant to observe daily production? +
Absolutely. In addition to touring our Ahmedabad manufacturing workshop, we regularly arrange guided client visits to operating customer plants across Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Madhya Pradesh so you can witness real daily throughput, autoclave steam cycles, and block packaging.
What documentation do you provide for bank loan approvals and DPR submissions? +
We provide comprehensive bank-ready Detailed Project Report (DPR) data packages, including machinery performance pro-forma invoices, utility consumption metrics (power, steam, water), raw material mass-balance calculations, and projected unit manufacturing costs per m³ required by banks and financial institutions.
Do you assist with civil foundation blueprints, shed design, and boiler piping? +
Yes. Our engineering scope includes full civil dynamic load GA drawings, foundation anchor bolt layouts, crane rail bridge elevations, autoclave pit cross-sections, and complete IBR steam piping / condensate return P&ID schematics.
