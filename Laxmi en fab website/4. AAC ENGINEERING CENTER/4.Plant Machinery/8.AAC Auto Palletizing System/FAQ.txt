What is an AAC auto palletizing system?
−
It is a post-autoclave material-handling line that coordinates the required transfer, separation, inspection, grouping, stacking and pack-preparation stages. The exact included operations depend on the agreed project scope.

Does every AAC plant need automatic palletizing?
−
No. The decision depends on peak finished-product flow, product mix, labour arrangement, pack format, available space, maintenance capability and project economics. Manual or selective automation may be more appropriate for some plants.

Is plant capacity enough to select the system?
−
No. Nominal daily capacity does not show how quickly products arrive after autoclaving. Peak discharge, cakes per handling window, cake dimensions and the required pack cycle must also be evaluated.

Does the palletizing system include block separation?
−
Not necessarily. Separation may be a separate machine, an upstream operation or part of an integrated line. The proposal should state the scope boundary clearly.

Can one system handle different AAC block sizes?
−
A project may be designed with multiple recipes, but supported dimensions, stack patterns, changeover methods and mechanical adjustments must be confirmed during engineering.

Can the system be installed in an existing AAC plant?
−
A retrofit may be possible after surveying the existing unloading method, space, foundations, floor levels, utilities, controls, stockyard movement and allowable shutdown period.

Does automatic palletizing eliminate all manual work?
−
No. Operators may still be required for inspection, rejected-block removal, packaging-material replenishment, forklift movement, cleaning, supervision and maintenance.

Does automation always reduce block damage?
−
No universal reduction should be assumed. Damage depends on incoming block condition, separation quality, alignment, gripping, movement, support and operating practice.
