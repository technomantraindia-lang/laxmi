Is the AAC block business profitable in India?
−
It can be profitable when saleable demand, realised price and capacity utilisation generate enough contribution to cover operating, fixed, finance and working-capital costs. Profitability must be tested for the proposed location and capacity.

How should AAC plant profitability be calculated?
−
Use saleable cubic metres—not only installed capacity. Revenue should be reduced by raw materials, utilities, manpower, maintenance, quality losses, selling expenses, freight support, overheads, finance costs and working-capital costs.

What is the Small Plant Trap?
−
A lower machinery investment can still produce a high cost per cubic metre when fixed operating expenses are distributed across limited saleable output. Small plants should be assessed through unit economics and expansion flexibility.

What is the Over-Investment Trap?
−
It occurs when installed capacity, automation or debt commitments materially exceed realistic market absorption and ramp-up. The result can be high break-even volume, working-capital pressure and prolonged underutilisation.

Is a fully automatic AAC plant always more profitable?
−
No. Automation should solve defined problems in labour, safety, consistency or capacity. It can reduce manual handling while increasing dependence on skilled electrical, instrumentation and automation support.
