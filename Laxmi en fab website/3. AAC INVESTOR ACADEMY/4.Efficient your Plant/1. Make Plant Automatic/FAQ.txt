Can every semi-automatic AAC plant be converted to fully automatic?
−
No. Feasibility depends on machinery condition, layout, cycle compatibility, utilities, controls, safety requirements, available shutdown and commercial payback.

Should the complete plant be automated at one time?
−
Not necessarily. A phased or hybrid upgrade may create better value when only selected operations are labour-intensive or production-constraining.

Will automation increase plant capacity?
−
It may improve actual output if avoidable waiting, handling or sequence losses are the constraint. It cannot increase output beyond the limits of the remaining production system.

Will automation reduce steam and electricity consumption?
−
Not automatically. Any saving must be linked to a measured source of loss and verified in kg steam per saleable m³ or kWh per saleable m³ under comparable conditions.

Does a fully automatic plant require less skill?
−
It may require fewer repetitive manual roles, but it normally needs stronger electrical, instrumentation and troubleshooting capability.

How should upgrade performance be accepted?
−
Agree the baseline, measurement method, operating conditions, exclusions, stabilisation period and acceptance criteria before implementation.
