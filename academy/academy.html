03 · SUPPLIER EVALUATION CRITERIA

The 8-Point Buyer Audit Framework. What every investor must verify before signing.

Machinery quotations can look identical on paper. Here are the 4 foundational engineering differences that determine whether your plant runs at 99% uptime or suffers chronic downtime.

01

Fabrication Tonnage & Heavy Steel Sections

Low-cost vendors use thin 6–8mm plate steel that warps under hydraulic pressure and repetitive cake cycling. Laxmi builds with 16–25mm structural steel with stress-relieved weldments.

  • Total plant structural weight >400 tons on 600 m³/day
  • Machined linear guide rails for zero-vibration mold travel
02

High-Speed Wire Cutting & Kerf Precision

Cutting accuracy determines block market value. Laxmi's multi-stage horizontal and cross-cutting machines utilize pneumatic wire tensioning for ±1.0mm IS:2185 dimensional tolerance.

  • Sub-60 second cutting cycle time with automatic wire cleaning
  • Zero bottom-waste slicing with continuous green cake recycling
03

Autoclave Shell Thickness & Steam Recovery

Autoclaves account for up to 40% of thermal operating costs. Laxmi autoclaves feature Class 1 pressure vessel certification, 16–20mm SA-516 Grade 70 boiler steel, and dual steam recapture.

  • 12–14 bar working pressure rating with quick-locking safety doors
  • Inter-autoclave steam blowdown transfer reducing boiler fuel by 18%
04

Centralized PLC SCADA & Chemical Dosing

Fragmented relay panels lead to density inconsistencies and cake collapse. Laxmi integrates centralized Siemens or Schneider PLC systems with high-precision load-cell batching.

  • 0.2% dosing accuracy on lime, cement, fly ash, and aluminum powder
  • Remote IoT telemetry for real-time cycle tracking & preventative alarms
