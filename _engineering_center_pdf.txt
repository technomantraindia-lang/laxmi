06 / INTEGRATION
Process + equipment + layout
This section connects the Engineering Center to the Machinery & Equipment page and keeps the site internally coherent.
Use a simple formula:
PROCESS  +  MACHINE  +  LOCATION  =  SYSTEM
1
PROCESS
What happens at this stage?
2
MACHINE
What equipment performs it?
3
LOCATION
Where is it in the plant?
Every process stage should offer a clear next link to the relevant machinery group. Every machinery group can point back to
the process and plant layout.
This avoids isolated pages and creates a clear content graph for both users and search engines.
