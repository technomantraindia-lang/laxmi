CRITICAL PROCESS BENCHMARK · SLURRY TEMPERATURE & ALUMINIUM TIMING

Aluminium powder initiates hydrogen gas generation ($2Al + 3Ca(OH)_2 + 6H_2O \rightarrow 3CaO \cdot Al_2O_3 \cdot 6H_2O + 3H_2 \uparrow$) within seconds of slurry contact. It must be dosed in the final 20–30 seconds of the batch cycle at exactly $40^\circ\text{C} \pm 2^\circ\text{C}$. Dosing too early causes hydrogen bubble loss inside the mixer; cold slurry retards rising, resulting in high-density sunken cakes.
