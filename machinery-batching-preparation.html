Batching & Preparation Modules

1. Continuous Wet Ball Mill

Heavy-duty manganese steel-lined cylindrical grinding mill loaded with forged high-chromium alloy steel balls. Grinds sand or pond ash into reactive slurry with fineness residue <15% on 45-micron mesh.

2. Slurry Storage Tanks & Agitators

50m³–100m³ cylindrical vertical slurry tanks fitted with planetary gear drive planetary agitators running 12–16 RPM to maintain solid suspension without slurry settling or segregation.

3. Multi-Component Digital Weighing Scales

Individual load-cell suspended hoppers for dry powder (cement, lime, gypsum), liquid slurry weighing vessel with pneumatic knife gates, and automated water dosing meters.

4. High-Speed Hydrodynamic Pouring Mixer

Dual-motion counter-rotating mixing blades delivering high shear turbulence. Slurry temperature is controlled ($40\text{–}45^\circ\text{C}$) before automated discharge into mould boxes.
