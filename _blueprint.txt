company. The homepage should communicate three things immediately: what Laxmi makes, how it helps
customers build AAC plants, and why the company is credible.

machine, batching unit, ball mill. Use natural language and only claim equipment that Laxmi actually supplies/manufactures.

Implementation checklist
09 - BUILD NOTES
Homepage build checklist
Use the current Laxmi logo and current company/contact information.
Keep hero text on the left and activity/video on the right.
Use actual Laxmi factory/equipment/project photos wherever available.
Do not use competitor photos or competitor statistics as Laxmi proof.
Place Solutions and Machinery & Equipment in dropdown navigation, not as oversized top-level clutter.
Keep AAC Investor Academy as a 5-category content system.
Use Projects as the strongest trust proof after commercial solutions.
Use Engineering Center for process and plant-layout visualization.
Add schema later: Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList and appropriate Product/Service markup once page data is...
Add internal links from homepage to every major commercial and educational destination.
Optimize images, use descriptive alt text, and keep hero video lightweight with poster image/mobile fallback.
Validate all current certificates, capacity claims, locations, phone numbers and product availability before launch.
Source discipline: this blueprint prioritizes the client-provided Laxmi brochure and current company information. Where the brochure is old or ambiguous, verify before publishing.
