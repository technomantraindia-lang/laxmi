01 · 2-AXIS CUTTING SEQUENCE

High-frequency oscillating wire cutting. Mirror-flat block faces with zero wire drag.

The Laxmi 2-axis cutting machine slices through upright green cakes without stopping or changing fixtures, completing horizontal thickness slicing and vertical length cuts in a continuous 3.5-minute sequence.

STAGE 01

Horizontal Oscillating Frame

High-tensile steel piano wires (0.6mm–0.8mm) oscillate axially at high frequency, slicing horizontally through the upright cake to create precision block thicknesses (75mm to 300mm).

STAGE 02

Vertical Cross-Cutting Frame

Rigid vertical frame with pre-calibrated wire spacing plunges cleanly downward through the cake, cutting standard block lengths (600mm / 625mm) and heights (200mm / 250mm).

Crust Milling & Profiling

CRITICAL PROCESS BENCHMARK · WIRE TENSION & OSCILLATION FREQUENCY

Static wire cutting causes wire bow under cutting resistance, resulting in concave block centers ($\pm 3.0\text{ mm}$ error). Laxmi's high-frequency axial oscillating wire mechanism slices through the green matrix with zero lateral deflection, achieving $\pm 1.0\text{ mm}$ tolerance and glass-smooth joint faces required for thin-bed mortar application.

03 · GREEN SCRAP RECOVERY

100% green waste recycling. Closed-loop slurry re-homogenization.

Trimmed top crusts and bottom scrap are never wasted. An integrated subterranean agitator pit instantly converts green scrap into high-grade return slurry with automated density blending.

01
Direct Crust Chute Catchment
Top and bottom crust trimmings drop automatically into heavy stainless steel chutes lined with water spray flushing jets.
02
High-Shear Agitator Re-slurrying Pit
High-speed disintegrator impeller rapidly breaks down tender green cake lumps into uniform liquid slurry in under 60 seconds.
03
Automated Return Pumping to Batcher
Heavy-duty slurry pump transfers homogenized green slurry back to the green return weigh hopper on the batching tower, achieving zero raw material waste.
