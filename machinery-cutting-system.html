CUTTING SEQUENCE & MECHANICS

High-Efficiency 2-Axis Wire Cutting

1. Horizontal Oscillating Wire Frame

High-tensile steel piano wires (0.6mm–0.8mm) oscillate axially at high frequency, slicing through green cake with zero dragging or surface tearing, achieving mirror-flat block faces.

2. Vertical Cross-Cutting Frame

Rigid vertical frame with pre-calibrated wire spacing plunges cleanly through the upright cake to create standard block lengths (600mm / 625mm) and heights.

3. Top Crust Trimmer & Bottom Scraper

Automated horizontal milling cutters skim the non-uniform rising crust from the top and bottom of the cake, ensuring sharp 90° edges on all six block faces.

4. Wet Green-Waste Recovery Sump

Trimmed green cake crusts fall directly into a water-flushed agitator trough below the cutter, re-slurrying waste and pumping it back to the batching mixer with zero material loss.
