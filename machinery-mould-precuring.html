01 · MOULD CIRCULATION & TRANSFER

Synchronized mould logistics. Heavy structural engineering for zero deflection.

The mould circulation loop forms the physical heartbeat of an AAC plant. Any dimensional warpage in mould boxes or side plates creates slurry leakage, uneven cake rising, or cutting wire breakage.

COMPONENT 01

CNC Precision Moulds

Fabricated from heavy channel sections and gusseted side plates, stress-relieved and CNC milled on all mating faces to ensure hermetic rubber seal contact and zero slurry bleed.

CRITICAL PROCESS BENCHMARK · PRE-CURING HARDNESS & PENETRATION

The green cake must reach a precise penetration resistance hardness ($0.18\text{–}0.25\text{ MPa}$, measured with a calibrated green penetrometer) before demoulding and wire cutting. Premature cutting causes wire collapse and block deformation; over-curing causes excessive wire drag, high wire breakage rates, and corner chipping.

03 · FERMENTATION TUNNEL DESIGN

Thermal enclosure engineering. Continuous rising stability through winter climates.

Laxmi pre-curing fermentation chambers maintain rock-solid thermal and humidity profiles, protecting the fragile rising cake from ambient drafts and seasonal cold fronts.
