Mould Circulation & Fermentation Circuit

1. Precision Mould Boxes & Side Plates

Fabricated from structural channel and reinforced plates, stress-relieved and CNC-milled on joint faces to ensure zero slurry leakage and exact geometric orthogonality.

2. Hydraulic Ferry Transfer Carts

Bi-directional traverser cars with built-in push-pull hydraulic chains that transfer mould trains across parallel pre-curing and return rail tracks smoothly.

3. Automated Mould Oiling & Cleaning Station

Motorized rotary scrapers clean internal walls before automated airless mist sprayers apply an ultra-thin layer of release oil to prevent green cake adhesion.

4. Insulated Pre-Curing Enclosures

PUF-insulated tunnel enclosures with automated steam radiator fin heating and humidity circulation fans to ensure consistent rising during harsh winter climates.
