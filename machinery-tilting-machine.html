ENGINEERING ADVANTAGES

Why Vertical 90° Tilting is Critical

Green AAC cake has low mechanical strength (0.15–0.25 MPa) before autoclaving. Flat horizontal cutting would cause horizontal wire drag and top cake collapse. Tilting the block 90° onto a sturdy side plate enables thin vertical wire slicing without compressive deformation.

Hydraulic Synchronous Cylinders

Dual proportional hydraulic cylinders with counterbalance valves ensure smooth rotational deceleration to 90.0° without mechanical shock.

Pneumatic / Hydraulic Lock Clamps

Precision guide clamps firmly lock the detachable side plate against the mould box before rotation begins, eliminating relative shift.

Overhead Crane & Spreader Frame

Rigid box-girder spreader beam suspended from an overhead crane transfers empty moulds back to the cleaning line in under 60 seconds.

Zero Edge Defect Guarantee

Precision machined alignment pins and guide rollers strip the mould vertically upward without dragging along green cake faces.
