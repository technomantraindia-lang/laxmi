01 · TILTING MECHANICS & PRINCIPLES

Why 90° vertical rotation is critical. Eliminating horizontal wire drag & bottom cake collapse.

Prior to autoclaving, green AAC cake has extremely low mechanical compressive strength (0.15–0.25 MPa). Flat horizontal slicing would cause horizontal wire drag and top cake collapse. Tilting the block 90° onto a sturdy side plate enables vertical wire slicing with zero lateral deformation.

FEATURE 01

Proportional Hydraulic Cylinders

Dual heavy-duty hydraulic cylinders with proportional valves and counterbalance manifolds deliver smooth acceleration and deceleration, preventing shock loads during the 90° swing.

CRITICAL PROCESS BENCHMARK · ROTATIONAL COUNTERBALANCE & DECELERATION

During the 90° swing, a 6.0m³ green cake weighs approximately 3.6 tons. If hydraulic flow is not proportionally decelerated at 85°–90°, the kinetic inertia will cause micro-shear fracturing within the green cake interior that only becomes visible as cracked blocks after autoclaving. Laxmi's closed-loop hydraulic counterbalance circuit completely eliminates inertia shock.

03 · SPREADER & CRANE SPECS

Rigid box-girder spreader beam. Overhead crane integration & return logistics.

The tilting spreader frame is suspended from a dedicated double-girder overhead crane, executing the demoulding cycle, lifting empty moulds, and returning them to the cleaning track in under 60 seconds.
