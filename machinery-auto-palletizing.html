Palletizing & Packaging Line Stages

1. Hydraulic Cake Separator (De-Sticking)

During high-pressure steam curing, adjacent block layers can lightly fuse at wire-cut interfaces. The hydraulic separator applies gentle, uniform tensile separation without edge fracture.

2. Gantry Block Stacker & Cube Builder

High-speed overhead servo gantry clamp lifts full block rows (up to 1.2m cube volume) and stacks them neatly onto standard wooden or plastic pallets.

3. Automatic Wooden Pallet Feeder

Magazine-loaded pallet dispenser feeds single empty pallets onto the roller conveyor synchronously as completed block cubes move forward.

4. Automated PET Strapping & Film Wrapping

Rotary stretch wrapper applies rainproof polyethylene stretch hood film, followed by dual-axis PET plastic strapping to secure packs for road transit.
