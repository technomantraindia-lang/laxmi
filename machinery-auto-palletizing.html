CRITICAL LOGISTICS BENCHMARK · LAYER DE-STICKING & TRANSIT STABILITY

Post-autoclave AAC blocks exhibit slight hydrothermal surface adhesion at horizontal cut lines. Forcing cubes apart manually causes severe corner breakages. Laxmi's hydraulic de-sticking separator applies uniform multi-point expansion before servo stacking, guaranteeing zero-defect export-quality packs.

03 · DISPATCH & EXPORT STANDARDS

High-speed yard logistics. Weather-proof protection & instant truck loading.

Properly palletized and strapped AAC blocks eliminate en-route shifting, transit damage disputes, and off-loading delays at construction sites.

01
High-Tension Dual-Axis PET Strapping
Automated strapping heads apply 19mm embossed PET plastic straps with up to 3,500 N tension, securing the block cube firmly against pallet stringers.
02
All-Weather Polyethylene Stretch Hooding
Tubular elastic stretch hood film covers the top and all four vertical faces, providing 100% UV protection and weatherproofing for outdoor storage.
03
Instant Forklift Yard Logistics
Standardized 1200mm × 1000mm pallet footprints enable twin-forklift trucks to load a 40-ton trailer in under 15 minutes with zero physical handling.
