REACTION 01 · AERATION FOAMING

Hydrogen Micro-Pore Expansion

2Al + 3Ca(OH)₂ + 6H₂O → 3CaO·Al₂O₃·6H₂O + 3H₂ ↑

Aluminium reacts with calcium hydroxide in the alkaline slurry, generating billions of evenly dispersed spherical hydrogen bubbles that double the green cake's volume within 25–35 minutes.

REACTION 02 · HYDROTHERMAL SYNTHESIS

Crystalline Tobermorite Formation

5Ca(OH)₂ + 6SiO₂ ──(190°C, 13 bar)──> 5CaO·6SiO₂·5H₂O

Under 12–14 bar saturated steam pressure at 190°C, reactive silica and calcium hydroxide synthesize into permanent, needle-like Tobermorite (11Å) crystals, giving AAC zero residual shrinkage and high load capacity.

02 · MANUFACTURING SEQUENCE

The 8 synchronized stages. Continuous high-volume production.

Every station in the Laxmi En-Fab production line is engineered for precise cycle synchronization, zero line bottlenecking, and 100% material recycling.

STAGE 01 · MILLING

Raw Material Milling & Slurry Storage

Fly ash or quartz sand is wet-milled in a continuous ball mill to <15% residue on a 45µm sieve. Slurry is stored in mechanical agitation tanks at 1.62–1.68 kg/L specific gravity.

Fineness: <15% / 45µm Density: 1.65 kg/L
STAGE 02 · BATCHING

Precision Electronic Load-Cell Weighing

Pneumatic feeding gates transfer cement, quicklime, and gypsum into digital load-cell weighing hoppers with ±0.5% precision. Slurry is dosed via automated electromagnetic mass meters.

Accuracy: ±0.5% SCADA Auto Dosing
STAGE 03 · MIXING

High-Shear Mixing & Mould Pouring

Constituents undergo intense hydrodynamic high-shear blending. Aluminium suspension is dosed in the final 30 seconds before automated pneumatic pouring into oiled mould boxes.

Cycle Time: 3.5–4.0 min High-Shear Impeller
STAGE 04 · PRE-CURING

Hydrogen Rising & Static Pre-Curing

Moulds circulate through climate-controlled pre-curing tunnels (40–50°C). The cake rises 2x and reaches 0.15–0.25 MPa green cutting strength in 120–180 minutes.

Tunnel Temp: 40–50°C Firmness: 0.20 MPa
STAGE 05 · TILTING

90° Hydraulic Tilting & Demoulding

A hydraulic tilting crane fixture clamps the side plate, smoothly rotates the green cake 90° upright, and strips the mould body away with zero vibration or edge crack stress.

Rotation: 90° Vertical Zero Edge Impact
STAGE 06 · CUTTING

2-Axis High-Precision Wire Cutting

Oscillating horizontal steel wires slice accurate block heights, followed by vertical cross-cutting with ±1.0mm tolerance. Top crusts are trimmed and 100% recycled into slurry.

Tolerance: ±1.0 mm 100% Waste Recycled
STAGE 07 · AUTOCLAVING

High-Pressure Hydrothermal Curing

Cakes cure in cylindrical autoclaves under 12–14 bar saturated steam pressure at 190°C for 10–12 hours. Computerized curves regulate vacuum, ramping, soaking, and blowdown.

Pressure: 12–14 bar Temp: 190°C (Saturated)
STAGE 08 · PACKAGING

Auto Palletizing & Film Strapping

Hydraulic separating clamps de-stick cake layers, robotically stack cubes on wooden pallets, and transfer them onto automated protective stretch-wrapping and strapping lines.

Cube De-Sticking Clamp Auto Film Wrapping
03 · VALUE-ADDED ENGINEERING

Specialized Operations for Reinforced ALC Panels.

Manufacturing load-bearing reinforced AAC wall, floor, and roof panels requires integrated steel reinforcement processing integrated directly into the mould line.

01. Anti-Corrosion Polymer Coating

Spot-welded steel rebar mesh cages (B500A grade) are dual-dipped in anti-corrosion latex dip and oven-cured before insertion.

02. Hydraulic Pin Suspension Fixture

Mesh cages are precision-suspended inside mould boxes using robotic cross-beams and alignment pins prior to slurry pouring.

03. Automated Pin Extraction

After hydrogen rising is complete and initial green strength is reached, suspension pins are hydraulically withdrawn before 90° demoulding.

04 · QUALITY ASSURANCE

In-line quality control. Conforming to IS 2185-3
and EN 771-4.

Every cubic meter produced is validated against rigid physical, mechanical, and crystalline standards to ensure compliance with Tier-1 infrastructure contracts.

TEST 01

Slurry Specific Gravity & Temperature

Hydrometer and thermocouple testing at pouring station ensuring 1.62–1.68 kg/L density and 38–42°C pour temperature.

Target: 1.65 kg/L (±0.02)
TEST 02

Green-Cake Penetrometer Hardness

Digital spring penetrometer verification before demoulding to ensure optimal cutting hardness without wire drift or collapse.

Target: 0.18–0.24 MPa
TEST 03

2-Axis Wire Dimensional Accuracy

Laser micrometer profiling of sliced green cake verifying ±1.0mm length, height, and thickness accuracy before autoclaving.

Tolerance: ±1.0 mm Max
TEST 04

28-Day Compressive & Density Testing

Oven dry density (450–650 kg/m³) and crushing strength testing on 150mm cubes per IS 2185 Part 3 specifications.

Strength: 3.5–5.0 N/mm²
