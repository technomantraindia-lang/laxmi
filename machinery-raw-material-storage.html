01 · MATERIAL STORAGE MATRIX

Engineering parameters by raw material. Silo sizing, aeration & conveying modes.

Different AAC powders exhibit vastly different bulk densities, flowability, hygroscopic tendencies, and aeration demands. Each storage vessel is purpose-engineered to prevent bridging, rat-holing, and moisture clumping.

CRITICAL PROCESS BENCHMARK · QUICKLIME HUMIDITY ISOLATION

Quicklime silos must feature hermetic isolation seals and high-velocity safety relief valves. Moisture ingress into unslaked lime triggers violent exothermic hydration ($CaO + H_2O \rightarrow Ca(OH)_2 + \Delta H$), generating massive volumetric expansion and localized heat that can rupture silo walls and ruin batch chemistry.

Heavy-duty structural fabrication. Engineered for 24/7 continuous dust-free feeding.
