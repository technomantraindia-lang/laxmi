The 8-stage AAC
production process story.

See how raw materials move through preparation, batching, pouring, precuring, cutting, autoclaving and packing to become finished AAC blocks and panels.

STAGE 01

Raw Material Prep

Siliceous materials (fly ash or sand) are wet-ground in heavy ball mills to a specific slurry fineness and stored in continuous-agitation tanks.

EQUIPMENT: Wet Ball Mill & Slurry Tanks
STAGE 02

Slurry & Binder Mixing

Fly ash slurry, OPC cement, quicklime powder, and gypsum are blended in high-shear mixers to form a uniform reactive slurry base.

EQUIPMENT: High-Shear Slurry Agitators
STAGE 03

Batching & Dosing

PLC-controlled SCADA dosing system meters precise weights of slurry, binders, water, and aluminum powder expansion agent (±0.5% accuracy).

EQUIPMENT: Automated Batching SCADA Tower
STAGE 04

Pouring & Moulding

The measured slurry mix is poured into precision oil-sealed steel mould boxes. Aluminum powder reacts with lime to produce millions of hydrogen micro-bubbles.

EQUIPMENT: Pouring Mixer & Mould Boxes
STAGE 05

Precuring & Rising

Moulds enter temperature-controlled precuring tunnels (40–50°C) for 2 to 3 hours as the cake expands to full volume and reaches green cake cutting hardness.

EQUIPMENT: Precuring Fermentation Tunnel
STAGE 06

High-Precision Cutting

Demoulded green cake is tilted 90° and passes through three-stage oscillating wire cutters for horizontal, vertical, and cross cuts with ±1mm accuracy.

EQUIPMENT: 3-Stage Wire Cutting Machine
STAGE 07

Autoclave Hydrothermal Curing

Cut blocks are loaded into ASME-certified pressure vessels for 10-12 hours of hydrothermal curing under 12 bar saturated steam (190°C) to form crystalline Tobermorite strength.

EQUIPMENT: Autoclave Pressure Vessels
STAGE 08

Cubing, Separating & Packing

Cured AAC blocks are separated, automatically stacked onto wooden pallets, and wrapped in heavy plastic film for dispatch.

EQUIPMENT: Auto Separator & Strapping Line
