02 · MANUFACTURING CIRCUIT

A synchronized circuit. Continuous 8-stage manufacturing.

Autoclaved Aerated Concrete transforms fly ash / sand and lime into lightweight structural masonry through precision dosing, hydrogen micro-aeration, and high-pressure crystalline hydrothermal synthesis. Eight synchronized stations ensure zero line bottlenecks.

01
Raw material milling
Fly ash or quartz sand is wet-ground in a continuous ball mill to a fineness residue <15% on a 45µm sieve, attaining 1.60–1.70 kg/L slurry density.
02
Batching & mixing
Electronic load cells weigh cement, quicklime, gypsum, and slurry with ±0.5% precision. Aluminium suspension is dosed into the high-shear mixer.
03
Mould pouring & rising
Slurry is poured into pre-oiled mould boxes. The alkaline reaction between aluminium and calcium hydroxide releases hydrogen micro-bubbles, expanding volume 2x.
04
Static pre-curing
Moulds circulate through climate-controlled pre-curing tunnels (40–50°C) for 120–180 minutes until the green cake achieves optimum cutting firmness.
05
90° tilting & demoulding
A hydraulic tilting crane locks the side plate, smoothly rotates the cake 90° upright, and strips the mould body with zero edge impact.
06
2-axis wire cutting
Oscillating high-tensile steel wires slice the green cake horizontally, followed by vertical cross-cutting with ±1.0mm precision. Waste crust is 100% recycled.
07
Hydrothermal synthesis
High-pressure saturated steam curing at 12–14 bar (190°C) for 10–12 hours converts reactive silica and lime into permanent Tobermorite crystal needles.
08
Auto palletizing & packing
Hydraulic clamps de-stick autoclaved cake layers, automatically stack blocks onto wooden pallets, and transfer them for protective film strapping.

For most new entrants, a 300 m³/day plant (upgradeable to 600 m³/day) offers the optimal balance between initial capital outlay, operational efficiency, and local market absorption within a 150 km delivery radius. Entry-level 150 m³/day setups are ideal for regional micro-markets with captive construction demand.
Automation should be strategically balanced. Core processes like PLC batching, raw material dosing, mould tilting, and wire cutting must be fully automated to guarantee ±1.0mm dimensional accuracy and zero cake breakage. Semi-automated palletizing and dispatch can be customized based on local labor economics.
The first step is conducting a local raw material assay (fly ash reactive silica and lime reactivity) combined with a 150 km market demand radius study. Laxmi En-Fab provides complete pre-feasibility modeling, plot layout blueprints, and project ROI calculations before machinery fabrication begins.
A standard 300 m³/day plant requires approximately 3.5 to 4.5 acres of land. The connected electrical load is roughly 450–550 kW with an operational power consumption of 22–26 kWh per m³ of finished block and 0.65 m³ water per m³ of AAC.
