01 · BOILER SIZING CALCULATION

Matching boiler TPH with autoclave schedules. Sizing for peak pressurization ramps, not average consumption.

An AAC steam boiler must be sized for concurrent peak steam demand during initial pressurization phases of multiple autoclaves. Sizing only for average hourly consumption leads to severe steam starvation.

CRITICAL PROCESS BENCHMARK · STEAM PRESSURE & TOBERMORITE SYNTHESIS

Sizing a boiler for average hourly steam consumption causes acute steam starvation when multiple autoclaves enter concurrent pressurization ramps. Saturated steam pressure dropping below $10\text{ bar}$ prevents complete tobermorite crystal synthesis, leading to high block drying shrinkage and sub-par compressive strength.

03 · UTILITIES & WATER TREATMENT

Boiler feed water conditioning. Preventing scaling & tube corrosion.

Raw groundwater contains dissolved calcium and magnesium salts that rapidly cause boiler scale and tube burnout if not demineralized.

01
Multi-Bed Industrial Water Softening
Dual ion-exchange softening resin beds reduce raw water total hardness to <5 ppm CaCO3, ensuring zero scale formation inside boiler tubes.
02
Atmospheric Deaerator Tank
Thermal deaeration removes dissolved oxygen ($O_2$) and carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) from feedwater, eliminating pitting corrosion in boiler shells and steam lines.
03
Automatic Continuous Blowdown System
Conductivity-controlled automatic blowdown valves vent concentrated TDS sludge while recovering thermal energy via a flash steam heat exchanger.
